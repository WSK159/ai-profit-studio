# The Painting of Ten Thousand Worlds — English World Bible (Condensed)

## The World

The realm of Chenhuan is a vast continent divided into five regions: Eastern Wasteland, Southern Marsh, Western Desert, Northern Plains, and the Central Province. Qi is richest in the center; the Eastern Wasteland is poor but full of ancient ruins.

Above this world lies the Upper Realm. For ten thousand years, no one has ascended—the Heavenly Road is broken. Legend says that at the break, an immortal left behind a painting: *The Painting of Ten Thousand Worlds*, which holds the secret to mending the road and glimpsing all worlds.

## Cultivation Realms

Qi Refining → Foundation Establishment → Golden Core → Nascent Soul → Spirit Severing → Void Refining → Unity → Mahayana → Tribulation Transcendence

## The Painting

Painted by the ancient immortal Emperor Xuandu from his own Dao fruit, the Painting contains fragments of ten thousand worlds in twelve scrolls: Perception, Suppression, Deduction, Absorption, Evolution, Longevity, Gate of Worlds, Reincarnation, Dao Sound, All Beings, Unity, and Transcendence.

It binds to a host as an ink mark on the palm. The stronger the host, the more scrolls unlock. If the host's heart breaks, the Painting devours their soul and becomes a blank page.

## Factions

- Qingyang Sect: one of the three great sects of the Eastern Wasteland; holds the Demon-Suppressing Tower and the Sword Tomb.
- Star-Plucking Tower: a secretive organization of killers and informants led by the mysterious "Sovereign of Fate," who collects fragments of the Painting.
- Xuanji Pavilion: neutral intelligence brokers.

## Key Items & Secrets

- Half a Qingyang Token: found by the protagonist in a buried cellar.
- The broken sword Qingwu: a sword-spirit remnant that chooses the protagonist.
- The Star-Shattering Order: the Sovereign of Fate's token.
- The Heavenly Road's broken end: the ultimate secret of the story.
