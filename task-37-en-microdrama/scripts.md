# The Billionaire's Fake Wife — 10-Episode Sample Script

**Logline:** A broke florist agrees to pose as a reclusive billionaire's wife for one month. He needs a cover. She needs rent money. Neither expects the contract to become real.

---

## Episode 1 — The Contract

**Hook (0-10s):** "Sign this, and for thirty days you're the richest woman in the city. Break the rules, and you owe me one million dollars."

**Scene A — Flower shop, day.** LILY (26) counts coins. Landlord's eviction notice taped to the counter.

**Scene B — Street.** A black car stops. Mr. LI (30) steps out with an assistant. "I've seen you work. You're honest. I need an honest wife."

**Scene C — Office.** Contract pushed across the table. Lily reads: "No falling in love."

**Close:** Lily signs. "Deal." Li's smile flickers—like he'd hoped she'd refuse.

---

## Episode 2 — Moving In

**Hook:** "The staff will call you Mrs. Li. You will call me 'darling' in public. In private—we don't exist."

**Scene A — Mansion.** Lily walks through a hall bigger than her old street. A maid curtsies.

**Scene B — Kitchen.** Lily can't sleep. She cooks at 2 a.m. Li appears in the doorway, watching. "That smells like my mother's soup."

**Scene C — Rooftop.** "Your mother?" "She died when I was nine." Lily says nothing. She pours him a bowl anyway.

**Close:** He drinks it. Something in his eyes softens. "Don't get used to it."

---

## Episode 3 — The Ex-Girlfriend

**Hook:** "I'm his fiancée. Whoever you are, you're temporary."

**Scene A — Gala.** A woman in red approaches Lily with a smile like a knife. "He never told you about me?"

**Scene B — Corner.** Lily asks, "Who is she?" Li: "A mistake from the past." "Then fix it, or I walk." He looks at her—no one talks to him like this.

**Scene C — Stage.** Li takes the mic, puts an arm around Lily. "Let me introduce my wife." The red dress cracks.

**Close:** In the car, Lily pulls away. "That was for the camera." Li: "Was it?"

---

## Episode 4 — The First Real Fight

**Hook:** "I don't need your charity. I need my dignity."

**Scene A — Kitchen.** Lily discovers Li secretly paid her landlord. She's furious.

**Scene B — Hallway.** "You lied to me." "I helped you." "You decided for me. That's different."

**Scene C — Night.** She finds him sitting alone in the dark. "Why do you care so much?" He doesn't answer. He almost says it.

**Close:** The next morning, a new vase of her favorite flowers. No note. That's his way of apologizing.

---

## Episode 5 — The Threat

**Hook:** "Someone followed my car today. I think they know about us."

**Scene A — Street.** Lily spots the same black sedan three times.

**Scene B — Office.** Li's assistant: "It's your brother. He wants the company, and he'll use her to get it."

**Scene C — Night.** Li moves Lily into his private wing. "Until this is over, you stay with me." "That wasn't in the contract." "Contracts change."

**Close:** Under the same roof, two doors apart, both of them lying awake.

---

## Episode 6 — The Brother's Trap

**Hook:** "I can pay you ten times what he's paying. All I need is one photo."

**Scene A — Café.** Li's brother, VICTOR, slides an envelope across the table.

**Scene B — Lily's choice.** She takes the envelope. Victor smiles.

**Scene C — Li's office.** She hands the envelope to Li, unopened. "He thinks I'm for sale. Prove him wrong."

**Close:** Li opens it—a photo of him and his mother, the only copy. His hands shake. "Thank you."

---

## Episode 7 — The Accident

**Hook:** "There's been an accident. Mrs. Li is in the hospital."

**Scene A — Hospital.** Li runs through the doors, tie undone, shouting her name. Staff have never seen him lose composure.

**Scene B — Bedside.** Lily wakes to find him asleep in the chair, holding her hand. She doesn't pull away.

**Scene C — Release.** "You didn't have to come." "Yes, I did."

**Close:** In the car, she asks softly, "How long were you awake?" "Since you signed the contract."

---

## Episode 8 — The Kiss

**Hook:** "One month ends tomorrow. I should return the ring."

**Scene A — Rooftop.** Lily tries to give back the ring. Li closes her fingers around it.

**Scene B — Rain.** "The contract is over." "Forget the contract." He leans in. She doesn't move away.

**Scene C — Midnight.** Their first real kiss, in the rain, no cameras, no audience.

**Close:** Her phone buzzes. A text from Victor: "So it's real. Now I really have you both."

---

## Episode 9 — The Press Conference

**Hook:** "This is not a confession. This is a choice."

**Scene A — Stage.** Victor has leaked everything. Cameras flash.

**Scene B — Li takes the stage.** "The story is true. I hired a wife. But somewhere in this month, I stopped pretending. I fell in love with her. That's not a scandal—that's my life."

**Scene C — Crowd.** Lily steps up beside him and takes his hand. "He forgot to mention I love him too."

**Close:** Victor's face, white with rage. But the crowd is cheering.

---

## Episode 10 — The Real Wedding

**Hook:** "This time, no contract. Just us."

**Scene A — Garden.** A small wedding. Old friends. The florist who trained Lily.

**Scene B — Vows.** "I, Lily, take you, Li, not because you're rich, but because you waited in a hospital chair for me."

**Scene C — Reception.** The mother's soup is served. Li's eyes shine. "She'd have liked you."

**Close:** Dancing under the lights, she whispers, "The first month was fake. The next fifty years are real." He smiles. "Deal."

---

## Production Notes

- 每集 60-90 秒，前 3 集免费，第 4 集起付费解锁
