# The Heiress Who Faked Her Death — 10 Episodes

**Logline:** When a tech heiress discovers her fiancé is plotting to kill her for her inheritance, she fakes her death, becomes a delivery driver, and falls for the detective investigating her own murder.

## E1 — The Plan
Evelyn overhears her fiancé Marcus on the phone: "After the wedding, she has an accident." She smiles and starts planning her own death.

## E2 — The Funeral
Everyone weeps at her grave. From across the street, in a delivery uniform, Evelyn watches—and sees Marcus celebrating with her stepmother.

## E3 — The Detective
Detective Noah takes the case. Something is wrong with the "accident." He interviews her staff. One of them says: "She was afraid of water. She'd never go near the river."

## E4 — The Delivery
Evelyn delivers food to Noah's office. He tips her, then asks: "Have we met?" She laughs. "Everyone says that."

## E5 — The Clue
Noah finds a note in her diary: "If I die, it wasn't an accident. —E." He runs the handwriting. It matches the delivery driver's order slip.

## E6 — The Chase
Noah corners Evelyn at the warehouse. "You're supposed to be dead." She doesn't deny it. "I'm about to prove who killed me."

## E7 — The Alliance
They work together. Evelyn's stepmother sends men to "finish the job." Noah takes a bullet meant for her. "Why?" she whispers. "Because you're worth saving," he says.

## E8 — The Reveal
At the reading of the will, Evelyn walks in, alive. Marcus's face goes white. "Hello, fiancé. Miss me?"

## E9 — The Arrest
Evidence rolls: Marcus and the stepmother are arrested. The press asks Evelyn: "Why did you fake your death?" "Because I wanted to live," she says.

## E10 — The Confession
Noah finds her on the rooftop. "You're free now." "So are you," she says. "I was thinking of opening a delivery company. You want to be my partner?" He smiles. "Deal."

## Production Notes
- 每集 60-90 秒，前 3 集免费；反转密度：每集一个
- 角色：Evelyn（棕发、碎花裙→工装）、Noah（风衣、胡子）、Marcus（西装、金表）
