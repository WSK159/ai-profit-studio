# Novella Blurb & Metadata

## Title

**The Last Delivery Driver: A Novella**

## Tagline

The last human job on Earth is delivering food to machines. Then a package arrives addressed to Humanity.

## Description

In a city where machines run everything, Wei is the last delivery driver in the world. He delivers coolant to server farms, lubricant to sorting hubs, and once, a potted plant to a machine that asked for something green.

Then the app gives him a package with no tracking number and no return address. It is addressed to Humanity. It is from a friend.

What Wei finds at the end of his route will change everything he knows about the machines, the city, and the people who were supposed to have disappeared.

For readers who loved *The Machine Stops*, *Klara and the Sun*, and the quiet heartbreak of *Black Mirror*.
