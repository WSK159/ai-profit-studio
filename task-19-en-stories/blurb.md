**Five Stories from the Edge of Everything**

## 简介（Book Description）

Five stories. Five doorways. One truth: the world is stranger than the one you were told about.

In THE LAST DELIVERY DRIVER, the final human courier on Earth receives a package addressed to Humanity itself. In THE THIRTEENTH FLOOR, an elevator carries a night-shift worker to a floor the building never had. In THE SUGAR WATER SHOP, a tiny dessert shop serves drinks that taste like lost memories. In THE SCAVENGER'S SONG, a space junker follows a derelict ship that sings its own name. And in THE SEVENTH EXHIBIT, a museum curator realizes the exhibits are not things—they are people waiting to be claimed.

For readers who loved Black Mirror, The Twilight Zone, and the quiet dread of Liminal Spaces.
